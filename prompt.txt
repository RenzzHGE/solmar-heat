Build SOLMAR HEAT, a complete open-world crime game for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty
("Make GTA 6", $1,666), as static files under game/ with no build step and no
network requests.

The game: a top-down open-world crime game in the original sun-soaked coastal city
of Solmar. A 7x7 street-grid city to explore on foot and by car. Traffic cars that
follow the road grid, turn at intersections and brake for the car ahead. Pedestrians
who walk the sidewalks and flee from danger. Cars to steal (walk up and press E;
the driver bails and runs). A wanted level of 0-5 stars that rises with crimes and
falls when the player stays clean; police cars that chase and ram, and foot cops
that chase and punch; getting caught on foot means BUSTED, running out of health
means WASTED. Three story missions in order from Mara the fixer — First Job
(drive to the pickup point), Hot Package (collect 3 packages, deliver to the
garage), Lose the Heat (reach the hideout and lose 3 stars of cops) — then free
roam with repeatable package runs for cash. Combat on foot (punch), car crashes,
health regen when the heat is off. A HUD with cash, wanted stars, a health bar, a
speedometer, a minimap of the whole city, a mission objective bar and an off-screen
target arrow. A start screen, pause, busted/wasted/win screens, and a save in
localStorage. Sound synthesised live with WebAudio (engine hum, two-tone siren,
action cues) — no audio files. Keyboard + mouse: WASD/arrows drive and walk,
Shift runs, Space handbrakes, E enters/steals/exits a car, F or click punches,
P pauses, M mutes, Enter starts/confirms.

Structure: game/index.html plus game/src/ split into one file per job — config
(tuning + palette), random (seeded RNG), utils (math/collision), input (keyboard
+ mouse), audio (WebAudio synth), city (seeded city builder: roads, blocks,
buildings, park, parking lot, beach), world (state + fixed 1/60s step + save),
player (on-foot + driving + stealing + punching + crashes), traffic (traffic cars
+ pedestrians), police (wanted system + pursuit), missions (3-job state machine),
render (canvas drawing: ground, cars, peds, HUD, minimap, menus), shots
(screenshot scenes staged by an autopilot that plays the real rules forward),
main (boot + fixed-step loop + ?shot= scene staging). main.ts at the repo root
copies game/ + screenshots/ to /output and writes /output/dossier.json with
buildDossier from vendor/makefun-game.ts (the kit's copy, unchanged). serve.ts
serves game/ locally. LICENSE is MIT.

Screenshots: 4 real renders of the rebuilt game in a headless browser at 1280x720
(start/title, driving, police chase at 3 stars, mission on foot), staged with
?shot=start|drive|chase|mission&seed=1. The first by name is the cover.

All names, art, sounds and music are original — nothing from GTA or any commercial
game. Polish counts: controls and a camera that feel right, effects, sound and
menus. The judge sees the game mostly through its screenshots, so each shot must
show the open world at its best: the city, the HUD with minimap, traffic, the
police after the player, a mission in progress.
